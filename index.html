Warranty & Process

The Melao’s building process

Four stages, roughly seven to nine months, with a named build superintendent from lot pin to key handoff.

  2. 01

    Lot Selection

    Walk the phase with a community manager. Compare orientation, tree cover, greenbelt backing and lot premium in writing.

    Week 1–2

  3. 02

    Plan Customization

    Pick your plan, then structural options and finishes at the design studio. Every selection is priced before it is locked.

    Week 3–6

  4. 03

    Quality Construction

    Slab, frame, mechanical, drywall, finish. Third-party inspections at four stages plus weekly photo updates in your portal.

    Month 2–8

  5. 04

    Walkthrough & Closing

    Two orientation walks, a punch list closed before funding, and a 30-day and 11-month warranty visit after you move in.

About Us

Texas roots, and a name on every door

Melao’s started in 2009 with one spec home in Schertz and a simple rule: build it like the family moving in is your own. Sixteen years and 1,480 homes later, we still run our own crews on framing and finish, and our superintendents live in the markets they build in.

We are not the biggest builder in Texas. We are the one that picks up the phone in year three.
